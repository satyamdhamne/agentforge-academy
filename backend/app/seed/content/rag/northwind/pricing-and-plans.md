# Pricing and Plans FAQ

## Plans
Northwind Cloud offers three plans. Starter costs 29 USD per month and includes 3 projects and 10 GB of storage. Business costs 199 USD per month and includes unlimited projects, 500 GB of storage and SSO. Enterprise pricing is custom and adds a 99.95% uptime SLA and a dedicated support engineer.

## Billing
Invoices are issued on the first day of each month. Annual billing gives a 15% discount. Customers can switch plans at any time; upgrades are prorated immediately and downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

## Refunds
Refunds are available within 14 days of the first payment on a new subscription. Duplicate charges are refunded automatically within 5 business days once reported to support.
