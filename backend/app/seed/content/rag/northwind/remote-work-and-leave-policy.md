# Remote Work and Leave Policy

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams agree on two shared office days each quarter and publish them in the team handbook. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a director and are reviewed every twelve months.

Remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems and must not work from public networks without it. Equipment purchased with the home-office stipend remains company property.

## Home-office stipend
New employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of 500 USD (or local equivalent) within their first 60 days. Receipts must be submitted through the expense system within 30 days of purchase.

## Paid time off
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year; carried-over days expire on March 31. PTO requests of more than 5 consecutive working days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave; secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Parental leave can be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
