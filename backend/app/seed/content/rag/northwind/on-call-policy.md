# Engineering On-Call Policy

## Rotation
Each service team runs a weekly on-call rotation that hands over every Monday at 10:00 local time. Engineers join the rotation after completing three shadow shifts.

## Response targets
The primary on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 5 minutes for SEV1 and SEV2 alerts and within 30 minutes for SEV3. If the primary does not acknowledge within 10 minutes the page escalates to the secondary, then to the engineering manager.

## Compensation
On-call engineers receive a stipend of 400 USD per week on rotation and one day off in lieu after any night with more than two pages.
