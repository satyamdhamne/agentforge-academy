# Engineering Compensation Bands 2026

## Bands
Level E3 base salary band is 95,000 to 125,000 USD. Level E4 is 120,000 to 160,000 USD. Level E5 is 155,000 to 205,000 USD. Level E6 is 195,000 to 260,000 USD.

## Review cycle
Compensation reviews happen once a year in April. Off-cycle adjustments require VP approval.
