# Data Retention and Deletion Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to customer data, employee data and system logs stored in any company system, including backups.

## Retention periods
Application logs are retained for 30 days. Security audit logs are retained for 1 year. Customer account data is retained while the account is active and deleted within 30 days after account closure. Backups are kept for 35 days and are encrypted at rest.

## Deletion requests
Customer data deletion requests must be completed within 30 days. Deletion must cover primary databases, search indexes and analytics copies; backups age out naturally within the backup retention window.

## Personal data in AI systems
Personal data must not be sent to third-party AI providers unless a data processing agreement is in place and the data is necessary for the task. Prompts and model outputs containing personal data follow the same retention period as application logs.
